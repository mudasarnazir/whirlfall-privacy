Whirlfall Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Whirlfall, an iOS game. Whirlfall does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Whirlfall stores gameplay progress, scores, unlocked content, preferences, and advertising eligibility choices locally on your device. This information is used to operate the game and is not sent to a Whirlfall-operated analytics server. You can remove locally stored information by deleting the app.

Advertising

Whirlfall uses AppLovin MAX to provide optional rewarded advertisements and occasional interstitial advertisements to eligible adult players. AppLovin and advertising partners configured through AppLovin MAX may process information such as:

  • device, operating system, language, and app information;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device or advertising identifiers, when available;
  • ad requests, impressions, clicks, and reward completion; and
  • diagnostic, fraud-prevention, and performance information.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, maintain service security, and personalize advertising where permitted. The exact information processed depends on your region, device settings, permissions, and the advertising configuration in use.

Learn more in AppLovin's Privacy Policy.

Tracking and privacy choices

On supported Apple devices, Whirlfall may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before allowing tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies. You can deny this request and continue playing. You can change the permission later in iOS Settings.

Eligible adult players choose between Personalized Ads and Limited Ads before advertising starts. Personalized Ads permits interest-based advertising and sale or sharing for advertising where applicable. Limited Ads declines interest-based advertising and opts out of sale or sharing for advertising. Whirlfall sends the corresponding privacy flags to AppLovin before the SDK initializes. This choice can be changed later in Whirlfall's Privacy & Ads screen. AppLovin also provides access, deletion, and opt-out tools through its privacy policy.

Children

Whirlfall is not directed to children. If a player selects that they are under 18, the AppLovin advertising SDK is not initialized and the game remains playable without advertising.

Sharing and retention

Whirlfall does not sell locally stored gameplay information. Advertising providers may process, share, and retain information as described in their own privacy policies and as required to provide their services or comply with law.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used for the information handled by Whirlfall. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Whirlfall's features, providers, or legal obligations change. The Last Updated date above will identify the latest version.
